# Project: Employee Retention Analysis & Prediction 

## Goal: Identify root causes of 16% attrition rate and build a risk model. Tools: Python, Pandas, Scikit-Learn, Random Forest.
جs
![Alt Text](https://github.com/AhmedElatwy/IBM-HR-Analysis/blob/50297bcab87380aa2ab6588f698d2a1336a228c2/Visuals/IBM%20HR%20Attrition%20Dashboard.png)

### Key Insights:

- Disproved the common belief that "lack of promotions" causes attrition.
  
  ![Alt Text](https://github.com/AhmedElatwy/IBM-HR-Analysis/blob/50297bcab87380aa2ab6588f698d2a1336a228c2/Visuals/attrition%20vs%20Promotion.png)

- Identified a "Toxic Zone" where Sales Representatives working Overtime with low pay had a 40% attrition rate.
  
  ![Alt Text](https://github.com/AhmedElatwy/IBM-HR-Analysis/blob/50297bcab87380aa2ab6588f698d2a1336a228c2/Visuals/What%20Drives%20Attrition.png)

- Built a predictive model (Accuracy: 88.8%) that flags high-risk employees before they quit.

### Recommendation: Increase base pay for Junior Sales Reps and cap overtime hours to reduce attrition by an estimated 15%.
