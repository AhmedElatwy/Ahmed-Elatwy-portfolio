# Logistics Bottleneck Analysis (Supply Chain)

## Domain: 
Operations & Logistics

## Business Problem:
An international logistics firm faced a 60% late delivery rate and needed to identify the root causes of delays to improve operational efficiency.

## My Approach:

o	Performed Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on shipping data to test hypotheses regarding shipping mode, warehouse blocks, and product importance.

o	Discovered a critical "Discount Cliff": Shipments with >10% discount were systematically deprioritized and delayed.

o	Identified a specific "cursed" weight range (1kg–3.5kg) correlating with high delay rates, suggesting automated belt bottlenecks.

## Key Results:
o	Provided specific recommendations to adjust shipping priority rules for discounted items, aiming to reduce the 60% delay rate.
